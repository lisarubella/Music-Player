## 🚀 Getting Started

### Step 1: Start the App
```powershell
cd "E:\Cute App"
npm run dev
```

Your app opens at: **http://localhost:5173**

### Step 2: Add Your Music
1. Look for the **"Add MP3 Files"** button (brown button with + icon)
2. Click it to open file browser
3. Select one or more MP3 files from your computer
4. Files appear in the playlist below

### Step 3: Play Music
1. Click any song in the playlist
2. Click the big **Play button** (brown circle) to start
3. Use controls:
   - **◀** Previous song
   - **▶** Next song
   - **Progress bar** to seek
   - **Volume slider** to adjust sound

### Step 4: Manage Your Playlist
- Click a song to play it
- Click **trash icon** (🗑) to remove a song
- Songs are numbered 1, 2, 3, etc.
- Active song is highlighted

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### "Add MP3 Files" button not working?
- Make sure to click directly on the button
- Try refreshing the page (F5)
- Check that your MP3 file isn't corrupted

### No sound playing?
- Check volume slider (should not be at 0%)
- Make sure speaker/headphones are connected
- Try a different MP3 file
- Check browser console (F12) for errors

### Songs won't delete?
- Click the **trash icon** 🗑 on the right side of song
- If that doesn't work, refresh and try again

### Playlist not showing?
- You need to add MP3 files first
- Click "Add MP3 Files" button
- Select MP3s from your computer
